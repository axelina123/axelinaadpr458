ABOUT

Resilient, Powerful, Determined, Inspiring

My story

Hi! I’m Axelina, I’m 26 and I’m a professional shot putter from Sweden. I’m an Olympic finalist and have competed at five World Championships. I’ve broken both the Swedish national record and the NCAA record, and in 2026 I won my first global medal, placing third at the Indoor World Championships.

How it started

My journey in track and field started when I was 7 years old, and I quickly fell in love with the sport. I began as a multi-event athlete before focusing on throwing at 15, and by 16 I had already competed internationally at the U18 European Championships. At 18, I moved away from home to train with one of Sweden’s top throwing coaches, Mats Olsson.

Nebraska

In 2021, I took the next step in both my athletic and academic career by moving to Nebraska to train under Coach Justin St. Clair. During my time at the University of Nebraska, I broke the NCAA, Big Ten, and school records, won 3 NCAA titles and 5 Big Ten Championships, and maintained a 3.9 GPA while earning a degree in Graphic Design and a Master’s in Journalism and Mass Communications with a specialization in Integrated Media Communications.

my passion

Today, I train professionally in Nebraska and continue to represent Sweden on the international stage, with my sights set on winning a medal at the 2028 Olympic Games in Los Angeles. Beyond my athletic career, I’m passionate about inspiring the next generation—especially young girls—to discover the power of strength training and throwing. Through my coaching, I aim to create a positive, supportive environment where athletes can grow with confidence, learn proper technique, and develop a love for the sport.
