ABOUT

Resilient, Determined, Inspiring


My story

Hi, my name is Axelina Johansson! I am a 26-year-old professional shot putter from Hagafors, Sweden. I am a 2024 Olympic finalist and have competed at the World Championships five times. I have both the Swedish shot put National record and the NCAA shot put record. I also won my first international shot put medal at the 2026 World Indoor Championships.

How it started

My track and field journey started as a multi-eventer at the age of 7 years old. At the age of 15, I began my journey as a thrower. By the age of 16, I was already considered an elite-level thrower and represented Sweden at the U18 European Championships. At the age of 18, I was offered an opportunity to move to Falun, Sweden, to train with one of Sweden’s elite national throwing coaches, Mats Olsson.



Nebraska

In 2021, I decided to take my athletic and academic career to the next level, and moved to Lincoln, Nebraska, to attend the University of Nebraska and train under shot put coach Justin St. Clair. While attending the University of Nebraska, I broke the NCAA, BIG 10 Conference, and the University of Nebraska’s shot put school record. I am a 3-time NCAA and 5-time BIG 10 Conference shot put Champion. While along this shot put journey at the University of Nebraska, I maintained a 3.85 grade point average earned a Bachelor’s of Art in Graphic Design and a Master’s of Science in Journalism and Mass Communications, with a specialization in Integrated Media Communications.

my passion

Today, I train professionally in Nebraska and continue to represent Sweden at international competitions, with my sights set on winning a medal at the 2028 Olympic Games in Los Angeles. Beyond my athletic career, I’m passionate about inspiring the next generation, especially young girls, to discover the power of strength training and throwing. Through my coaching, I aim to create a positive, supportive environment where athletes can grow with confidence, learn proper technique, and develop a love for the sport.
