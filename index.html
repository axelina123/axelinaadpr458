About Me

Hi! I’m Axelina, an Olympic shot putter from Sweden. I’ve competed at five World Championships, broken both the Swedish national record and the NCAA record, and in 2026 I won my first global medal, placing third at the Indoor World Championships. I’m passionate about training hard, building strength, and constantly pushing my limits.
